# Tiny Airways privacy policy

Tiny Airways is a single-player game. It does not collect, sell or share any personal information.

- **No accounts, ads or analytics.** The game has no sign-in, shows no ads and sends no usage data anywhere.
- **Your save game** is stored on your device and, if iCloud is turned on, synced between your own devices through Apple's iCloud. Only you and Apple can see it, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Beta feedback.** While testing through TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback and crash reports you choose to send, along with the email address on your TestFlight invite, with the developer. They are used only to fix and improve the game.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/paulkinderman/tiny-airways/issues>, or use the feedback email shown in TestFlight.
